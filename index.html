The phone rings. Nobody picks up. The job goes next door.

An AI receptionist answers when you're on a roof, off the clock, or buried in work. It books the visit and emails you the details.

Three steps, no new phone number

  1. Forward the line. Your carrier sends missed and after-hours calls over. The number on your truck stays the same.
  2. The assistant answers. It says it's an assistant, then asks what the job is and takes the details.
  3. You get the lead. An email arrives as soon as the call ends, and the call goes in a log with a short summary.

Not built for emergency dispatch or life-safety lines, outbound sales calling, or large call centers.
